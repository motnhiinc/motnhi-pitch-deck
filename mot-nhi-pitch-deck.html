Solana Mobile

A phone for daily life.
A place for family reading.

Our thesis: a family reading routine gives parents a reason to return.

A focused consumer use case

Vietnamese stories for parents with compatible phones. A daily use beyond crypto.

A practical distribution path

Test on Seeker, then seek publication in the Solana dApp Store.

A family-first experience

Children explore books. Any future wallet or purchase step stays with parents.

Market wedge

Start with one audience.
Earn the next story.

Vietnamese parents aged 25–40 in cities, with children ages 3–8, who already buy books.

First occasion: a short shared story at home.

Reach families through books

Recruit through parent communities, bookstores, preschools, and publisher demos.

Test two audiences separately

Test reading habits with Vietnamese families and device fit with Seeker parents.

Vietnam family demand does not imply Seeker adoption.06 / 9
Business model

Prove repeat reading.
Then earn the purchase.

Start with a trial

Let families finish a first story before asking them to pay.

Test paid story access

Compare story packs with family library access. Packaging and price remain unvalidated.

Grow through partners

Explore licensed book bundles with publishers and bookstores once family demand is demonstrated.

The parent pays for a useful reading experience. The child gets a story worth returning to.

Revenue hypotheses · no revenue or pricing claim.07 / 9
Pilot plan

Measure reading.
Not just downloads.

Recruit 20 parent testers and two pilot partners. Observe the first story, then follow up in week two.

≥60%

Target: completed first story within 24 hours of starting the trial.

≥30%

Target: return in week two. Ask whether families actually read together again.

≥10%

Target: trial-to-paid, or strong documented willingness to pay.

Targets, not results. Also check parent effort, preparation delays, content fit, and Seeker performance.
