Problem
Screens win attention

Story time should not have to lose.

Parents want children to enjoy books, yet digital experiences are often more immediately rewarding. Today’s trade-off is too blunt: passive screen time or a paper book that may struggle to hold attention.

“I want healthier screen time.”The parent need: technology that guides children back to a meaningful activity.
“I still want us to read together.”The design constraint: the app must support the parent–child ritual, not replace it.
“Vietnamese should feel native.”The local gap: voice, stories, and onboarding made for Vietnamese families.
Problem framing from the GTM and brand foundation documents.02 / 10

Business model
Monetization to validate—not pretend

Three revenue paths. One pilot decides.

1Hypothesis

Family access

Trial-to-paid access for a growing library of interactive stories. Pilot interviews determine packaging and willingness to pay.

2Hypothesis

Story packs

One-off or bundled story unlocks for families who prefer ownership over a recurring subscription.

3Hypothesis

Partner programs

Publisher, bookstore, and preschool packages that pair content, live demos, and family acquisition.

Pricing is intentionally absent: the roadmap requires willingness-to-pay evidence before a model or price point is presented as fact.
Decision rule: monetize the behavior families repeat—not the feature they merely try.07 / 10
Go-to-market
A six-week learning engine

Show the magic. Earn trust. Measure return.

Short-form reveal

4–5 weekly videos: book → AR, child reactions, parent pain points.

Community trust

Value-first posts, live demos, and early-tester recruitment.

Partner proof

Two preschool, bookstore, or publisher pilots with QR demos.

Referral loop

Unlock bonus stories only after week-two retention is proven.

Review weekly. After launch, double down on the two channels that produce activated and returning families—not the most views.
Product Hunt, directories, and hackathons support launch visibility; they are not the core engine.08 / 10
