# Mọt Nhí deck source of truth

Last reconciled: 12 October 2026.

## Canonical product facts

- Name: **Mọt Nhí**. Use **Mot Nhi** only where technical fields reject Vietnamese diacritics.
- Tagline: **“Chạm vào trang sách, bước vào câu chuyện.”**
- Product: an interactive reading app for Vietnamese children aged 3–8 that adds AR and Vietnamese read-aloud to paper books.
- Product principle: the screen is a bridge back to the book; it does not replace the paper book or the parent’s role.
- Canonical activation event: **completed first story**.
- Primary ICP: urban Vietnamese parents aged 25–40 with children aged 3–8 who already buy books or learning products and want healthier screen time.
- Secondary audiences: preschools, bookstores, publishers, and parenting communities.

## Validation targets—not traction

- 300 qualified waitlist sign-ups.
- 100 trials.
- At least 60% complete the first story within 24 hours.
- At least 30% return in week two.
- At least 10% trial-to-paid conversion, or strong documented willingness-to-pay evidence.
- 10 usable testimonials/UGC assets.
- Two active distribution partners.

The deck must never present these as achieved results until founder-reported evidence is supplied with an as-of date.

### Problem evidence and boundaries

- GSO/UNICEF Viet Nam SDGCW 2020–2021, Early Childhood Development snapshot, p. 2: 27% of children under five had three or more children's books at home. https://www.unicef.org/vietnam/media/8681/file/Early%20Childhood%20Development.pdf
- This is book access, not reading frequency, literacy, or an estimate for all children ages 3–8. The initial buyer segment already purchases books; the app does not solve lack of physical book access by itself.
- Busy parents struggling to sustain shared reading is the founder's customer hypothesis to test, not a quantified national survey finding. Avoid saying Vietnamese parents do not care for their children.
