# ADR 0006 — Funding, traction, and go-to-market slides

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19
- **Sources:** Mermail Notion page “Funding” (prepared 2026-08-16) and founder-reported traction supplied 2026-08-19

## Decision

The primary investor deck now uses a 10-slide structure and adds dedicated slides for traction, business model and go-to-market, and funding allocation.

### Traction baseline

The deck reports 760 users, 5,231 emails sent, 643 active workspaces, 153 connected wallets, and 45 Agent Wallet users. Social proof is reported separately as 980 X followers, 100+ Discord users, and 40 LinkedIn users. These figures are labeled founder-reported and dated 19 August 2026.

### Business model and GTM

The deck presents $19 entry, $59 builder, and $149 team/usage packages as pricing tests rather than validated price points. The GTM sequence is design partners, paid activation, and repeatability. The initial wedge stays bounded to one agent client, one payment provider, one region, and one SaaS subscription workflow.

### Raise and allocation

The ask is $250,000 pre-seed for an eight-month proof plan. Allocation is 52% product, engineering, and founder runway; 18% paid activation and design partners; 10% security, risk, and compliance; 10% protected contingency; 6% email, payment, and cloud infrastructure; and 4% legal, finance, and operations.

The deck keeps the $25,000 reserve outside routine operating burn and shows the planned $28,125 average monthly operating burn for the remaining $225,000.

## Consequences

- The former Product Progress slide is replaced by the metric-led traction slide.
- The former Business Model slide becomes Business Model & GTM.
- Funding allocation is separated from the team slide so the six-bucket plan remains legible.
- The final Team & Ask slide focuses on founders and proof milestones rather than repeating allocation bars.
- All future traction refreshes must preserve the reporting date and distinguish founder-reported, internal, design-partner, and external production activity.
